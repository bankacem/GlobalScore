A midfield role built on movement

Ødegaard’s influence often appears before the final pass. By adjusting his position around a compact block, he can offer the ball carrier another angle, draw a defender and help the next player receive with more time.

What to watch in the next match

The useful questions are practical: does his movement create a lane for a winger, does the next pass change the point of attack, and can Arsenal stay connected after losing possession? These are observations rather than predictions.
